# The Tally and Statutory Form images are published publicly

The Tally and the captured Statutory Form images are published to the public,
read-only, by ward, constituency and county. Where two organisations publish
Claims for the same Polling Station and agree, the Public Tally shows
"confirmed by N independent organisations"; where they disagree, it shows
"disputed" with both images.

## This reverses an earlier assumption

The source PRD sold exclusive, private access to captured data as the
subscription's value. That is now reversed. Agreement between rival parties on
the same Station Result is the strongest signal this platform can produce, and
it is only possible if publication is the default. Exclusivity would have
destroyed the product's central claim in order to protect a pricing model.

## Consequences

- Every page carries "Tally, not a Declared Result" and shows coverage, in
  compliance with Elections Act s.39(1G), under which published figures are
  "for purposes of public information only" and cannot found a declaration.
- Publication requires masking of personal data — see ADR-0009.
- Observer organisations are served free or at cost, because their independent
  confirmations are what make the Public Tally believable.
- Pricing can no longer be sold on exclusive data access. One published price
  list applies to everyone; uniform public pricing is itself a neutrality
  signal.
- Legal review precedes launch, and precedes any station-level finding being
  published.
